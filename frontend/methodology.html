Detection Methodology

How PhishGuard analyzes URLs and identifies potential phishing threats.

Analysis Pipeline

PhishGuard performs a multi-stage analysis for every URL submitted. Each stage contributes findings that feed into the final risk assessment.

1

URL Validation & Analysis

The URL is validated, normalized, and analyzed for structural indicators such as excessive length, suspicious characters, IP address hosts, encoded content, URL shorteners, and domain entropy.

2

DNS & Domain Analysis

Domain registration data is retrieved via RDAP/WHOIS to check domain age, registrar, and registration status. DNS records (A, AAAA, MX, NS, SPF, DMARC) are examined, and ASN/geolocation data is collected.

3

HTTPS & TLS Inspection

The TLS certificate is validated, checking for self-signed certificates, hostname mismatches, expiration, and issuer credibility. TLS version and cipher strength are also evaluated.

4

Redirect Analysis

Redirect chains are followed safely, checking for cross-domain redirects, HTTPS downgrades, URL shortener expansion, meta refresh redirects, and JavaScript-based redirections.

5

Webpage Structure Analysis

HTML content is parsed to detect login forms, password fields, external form actions, hidden elements, iframes, suspicious script patterns, and brand impersonation signals.

6

Brand Detection

Known brand names are checked against the URL, page title, visible text, favicon, and form actions. Typosquatting detection uses edit distance, homoglyph analysis, and combosquat patterns.

7

Machine Learning Model

A trained model evaluates host and lexical features extracted from the URL to produce a phishing probability score. The model output is one component of the final risk assessment.

8

Rule Engine & Risk Scoring

Expert-crafted rules detect multi-signal combinations. The risk engine combines ML probability, rule findings, webpage signals, and domain trust into a final 0-100 risk score with classification.

Machine Learning Model

The ML component uses host-lexical features extracted from the URL structure. Model metrics are loaded from the trained model artifact.

Risk Scoring

The final risk score (0-100) is computed as a weighted combination of available analysis components:

  • ML Component: Phishing probability from the trained model
  • Rules Component: Points from URL, redirect, and rule findings (saturated)
  • Webpage Component: Points from webpage and brand analysis findings
  • Domain Component: Points from domain, TLS, and reputation findings

Classification thresholds:

LEGITIMATE
Risk score below 30 (Low band)
SUSPICIOUS
Risk score 30-59 (Moderate band)
PHISHING
Risk score 60-79 (High band)
PHISHING (Critical)
Risk score 80+ (Critical band)

Rule Engine

Rules detect multi-signal phishing patterns that individual checks might miss. A single finding never pushes the classification to PHISHING by itself -- only combinations of signals can.

Limitations

  • This tool provides an automated technical assessment and should not be treated as definitive proof of legitimacy or maliciousness.
  • Phishing sites can be taken down or modified after analysis. Results reflect the state at the time of scanning.
  • The ML model was trained on a specific dataset and may not recognize novel phishing techniques not present in the training data.
  • Domain registration data may be incomplete or unavailable for certain TLDs or registrars.
  • Pages behind authentication, CAPTCHAs, or bot-detection systems cannot be fully analyzed.
  • A valid HTTPS certificate does not guarantee trustworthiness -- many phishing sites use valid certificates from free providers.
  • Newly registered legitimate domains may receive higher risk scores due to lack of established reputation.
  • The tool does not submit forms, bypass access controls, or interact with page functionality beyond passive observation.
